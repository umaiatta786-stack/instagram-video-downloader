Frequently asked questions

How do I download an Instagram video?

Copy a supported public video or Reel URL, paste it into the field, and select Analyze link. If the server can retrieve and verify the post, a download option will appear.

Can I download Instagram Reels?

Public Reel links are accepted. Whether a particular Reel can be retrieved depends on Instagram's access restrictions and the current retrieval method.

Can I download videos in HD or 4K?

Only when the source actually offers that quality. This tool does not upscale lower-resolution media or promise 1080p or 4K for every video.

Do I need an app or an account?

No separate app or sign-up form is required to use this website. Instagram private content is not unlocked by this tool.

Can I download a private Instagram video or Story?

No private-content access is provided. Stories, older IGTV links, and restricted content may be unsupported or unavailable.

Does this tool convert video to MP3 or remove watermarks?

No. MP3 conversion and watermark removal are not offered in this version. The site does not claim to remove a watermark or create audio files.

Why is my Instagram link not working?

The post may be private, deleted, age-restricted, region-restricted, temporarily unavailable, or blocked by Instagram. Try a supported public Reel or video post URL.

Is the original video quality preserved?

The downloader requests the best compatible source media it can retrieve. The actual available quality depends on the source; it is not guaranteed to be the original upload quality.
